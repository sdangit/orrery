Reference grid

The grid stays parallel to the system XY plane, even when the camera is aligned to a selected orbit. Blue on the +Z side, orange on the −Z side.

Follow the view center for close-ups, or pin the plane to the system center. Zoom in for finer divisions.

Orbital group centers

Faint × markers show group centers. The selected group is brightest; the camera-focused group has medium brightness. Inactive markers appear as the bodies separate on screen.
